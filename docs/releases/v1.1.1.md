# Spire Gym 1.1.1

Version 1.1.1 is a gameplay-fix release. A stress campaign after 1.1.0 played
many seeded runs across characters and ascensions and found twelve gameplay
faults. Each fix is in the shared engine and follows the original game.
Observation, action and descriptor layouts are unchanged, and no Python names
are added or removed. The rules IDs change, so records made with 1.1.0 rules no
longer replay. Rust is still the only simulator.

## Gameplay fixes

Most of the cases below used to stop with an error; the others gave a result
that differed from the original.

- **End of combat.** Escaping with Smoke Bomb now ends the battle the way a win
  does, so the victory relics Burning Blood, Black Blood, Meat on the Bone and
  Face of Cleric now fire (Self Repair already did). Rewards are still
  withheld. An earlier rule that skipped these relics on an escape was wrong,
  and its tests are corrected. Meat on the Bone also now checks its half-HP
  threshold before the other victory heals, as the original does. An Ironclad
  winning at 40/80 with Burning Blood now ends at 58 HP instead of 46.
- **Card cost.** A card's cost no longer depends on having a target. Infernal
  Blade played while no enemy could be targeted, such as during Awakened One's
  rebirth, used to fault. See the behaviour change below.
- **Time Warp.** Time Warp's twelfth card can now be one that Distilled Chaos
  plays, directly or through a copy (Double Tap, Burst, Echo Form, Amplify,
  Necronomicon) or Havoc. Its remaining queued cards are dropped, enemies gain
  Strength and the turn ends, as for other automatic plays. A dropped card is
  cleaned up as if it had been played: a Power leaves the piles, and Strange
  Spoon can roll for an exhausted card.
- **Events and relics.** Omamori can block a curse produced by an event's
  transform (Living Wall, Drug Dealer, Transmogrifier); the chosen card is
  still removed. With Ectoplasm, Tomb of Lord Red Mask's gold is now simply
  not gained, as in the original.
- **Enemies.** An enemy dies only once. A poison tick no longer kills an enemy a
  second time after another hit has killed it. A Bronze Orb holding a Stasis card
  returns that card once. Against the Heart, The Boot now raises a small hit
  before Invincible caps the damage, so Invincible can no longer be overspent.
- **Choices and hand.** A repeated card (Burst, Echo Form, Amplify,
  Necronomicon, Duplication Potion) waits for an earlier card's open choice and
  then plays. Nightmare now works when Foresight's Scry opens first at the start
  of a turn. At the end of a turn, retained cards return to the hand before Dead
  Branch, Dark Embrace and Feel No Pain act, so the hand never goes above 10
  cards.

## Behaviour change: costs when nothing can be targeted

Some moments have no targetable enemy, for example the last step of a won combat
or Awakened One's rebirth. Observations now show card costs at those moments: the
displayed `effective_cost` is known instead of unknown, and the layout is
unchanged. Effects that read or change a card's cost now act there too, as in the
original. They include Snecko Eye and Confusion, Snecko Oil, Mummified Hand,
Madness, Enlightenment, Setup and the end-of-turn reset of temporary costs.
[CHANGES](https://github.com/klottick/spire-gym/blob/v1.1.1/CHANGES.md) has the
full list. Runs through these moments can therefore differ from 1.1.0. For
example, a card drawn under Confusion now gets a random cost there, which also
advances the card RNG. Separately, the draw-pile views of Watcher's Evaluate,
Wish and Reach Heaven now show costs in every window; that change is
observation only.

| Standard | `spire-gym-native-standard-rules-v5` | `spire-gym-native-standard-rules-v6` |

Records with an older rules ID fail closed with an explicit error; there is no
multi-version replay. All frozen fingerprints are unchanged, including the
compact one: Standard Run `bf8e1d11…`, compact Standard Run `633b9424…`,
Standard combat view `5d8147e1…`, card metadata `effbb810…`, M9 `270163f7…`,
M10 Heart `dccc7624…`, M10 policy `28d92d89…`, Gym66 `4c73f7de…` and
`5ba729fd…`, and combat-v1 `8fd66d21…`. The frozen replay digests and the
combat-v1 digest are unchanged. Because costs are now shown when nothing can be
targeted, four Heart pins (Ironclad A20, Silent A0 and A20, Watcher A20) and six
of the eight `standard-combat-v1` agreement digests were re-pinned; the displayed
cost is the only cause. The four Heart routes take the same steps and reach the
same end and return. The `build_info()` keys are unchanged.

## Verify a wheel

1. Import the extension from the target environment and record
   `spire_lab._native.__file__` and `spire_lab._native.build_info()`.
2. Check that `dirty` is `false`, `features` is `["spire-python/extension"]` and
   `profile` is `release`. Then check that `source_digest` and `file_digest`
   match the values published with this release. The version bump alone changes
   `source_digest`, so 1.1.0 values do not carry over.
3. Check the wheel and `.so` SHA-256 values published with the release, then
   update consumer pins. Rules-ID pins must move; fingerprint pins stay.

The digests and artifact hashes are published with the GitHub release, not in
this file. There is no PyPI release.

## Known differences and limits

- **Havoc card order (open).** When Havoc is copied (by Burst or Duplication
  Potion, for example) or played by Distilled Chaos, the order in which cards
  play still differs from the original. For a copy, the original plays the copy
  of Havoc before Havoc's top card; the engine plays the top card first. The cases
  that used to fault now complete. The difference is tracked for a later
  fix.
- **Maximum HP and counters (newly documented).** A Run's maximum HP is capped at 999, and so
  are relic and power counters such as Self-Forming Clay's pending Block. Going
  past a cap stops with a typed `Unsupported` fault; the original has no cap.
- **Potion timing (newly documented).** Potions are offered only between decisions. The
  original also allows Distilled Chaos while Time Warp's twelfth card is still
  resolving; that ordering cannot happen here and is not modelled.

The 1.1.0 and 1.0.0 limits still apply. See
[delivery limits](https://github.com/klottick/spire-gym/blob/v1.1.1/docs/delivery.md#7-known-limits-by-surface).

The fixes in this release came from a stress campaign. More hardening may follow
in later patch releases.
