# Spire Gym 1.2.3

Version 1.2.3 expands public-information combat search on Standard. It covers
more exact draw states and supports search roots under Runic Dome. These are
stages 2 and 3 of spire-61l.

## Exact draw knowledge (PR #126)

Public search now accounts for retired repeat-copy families when the remaining
drawable card can act independently of its retired relatives. Canonical roots
and research samples carry forward public changes to card classes and combine
agreeing top and bottom anchors that refer to the same card. This includes
grid picks of a unique card class.

In a natural A20 corpus, draw-knowledge refusals fell from 2,562 to 51 across
83,852 decisions. See the [search scenario profile](../milestones/m34s-search-scenario.md).

## Runic Dome search roots (PR #127)

Runic Dome hides enemy intents. Search reconstructs each pending hidden move
from public pre-roll records, including executed moves, public overrides and
the context at the time of the roll. Canonical roots and research samples run
move selection with their own streams. The live roll result stays outside
the record.

The share of Runic Dome decisions that produced a root rose from 0% to 94.37%
across 84,732 measured decisions. Three new refusal reasons describe decisions
the public record cannot support.

| Code | Reported reason |
| --- | --- |
| `HiddenIntentRead` | `a card read a hidden intent` |
| `HiddenPrerollShown` | `a hidden pre-rolled value was shown by damage` |
| `HiddenMoveAmbiguous` | `an executed enemy move was not identifiable` |

See [public belief and search](../standard-run-api.md#public-belief-and-search-m34s-b).

## Identities and compatibility

Version 1.2.3 is a drop-in upgrade from 1.2.2. Existing records can be reused
as-is. Gameplay, observation, action, descriptor and root layouts, frozen
fingerprints, frozen replay digests and Heart pins are unchanged. Previously
supported roots keep identical digests. The search and belief contracts and
the `build_info()` keys stay at their current versions.

| Profile | Rules ID |
| --- | --- |
| Standard | `spire-gym-native-standard-rules-v9` |
| M10 Heart and Gym66 | `spire-gym-native-m10-rules-v6` |
| M9 | `spire-gym-native-m9-rules-v7` |

The version bump changes the extension's `source_digest` and `file_digest`
inputs. See the [1.2.2 fingerprint table](v1.2.2.md#current-frozen-and-compact-fingerprints)
for the unchanged pins.

## Verify a wheel

1. Import the extension from the target environment and record
   `spire_lab._native.__file__` and `spire_lab._native.build_info()`.
2. Check that `dirty` is `false`, `features` is `["spire-python/extension"]`
   and `profile` is `release`. Match `source_digest` and `file_digest` to
   the values published with the release.
3. Verify the wheel and `.so` SHA-256 values published with the GitHub release.

The wheel is distributed through GitHub Releases.

## Known differences and limits

- Under Runic Dome, once a Darkling's Nip or a Louse's Bite reveals its
  pre-rolled value through damage, roots refuse for the rest of that enemy's
  life. A truncated Darkling attack also triggers this refusal. Maw's
  conservative ambiguity rule still applies when public damage could match
  either Nom or Slam and a pending roll depends on which move executed.
  Roots also refuse while a card's read of a hidden intent is still relevant
  or when the public record cannot classify a change.
- Search still refuses drawable copies whose purge disposition is unpublished,
  such as an Anger copy produced by a purged repeat. Random-target autoplay
  and its repeats remain unsupported. Picks among identical draw-pile copies
  can still refuse, including with two-pick Seek+ or Frozen Eye.
- Omniscience's two selected-card plays can resolve ahead of waiting cards.
  In the original game both plays join the back of the queue. The engine can
  finish a Power's cleanup before Storm channels Lightning. The original
  queues Lightning before cleanup.
