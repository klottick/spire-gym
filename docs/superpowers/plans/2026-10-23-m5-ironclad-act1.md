## Review Focus

1. Full-map coverage versus hidden cuts: every legal generated room/edge stays playable for the named profile; default standard pools stay incomplete and no future encounter/status/private draw order leaks.
2. Reactive bosses: Guardian's surviving-HP threshold and bottom-queued transition, Sharp Hide once per Attack play, Hexaghost's captured Divider and staged Burn mutation preserve queue ordering, ownership and retained faults.
