## Review Focus

These five conditions follow from the design but are easy to miss. Each has a test in
its owning task.

1. **Finite stock already exhausted by Act3.** An Act3 elite or chest when every pool
   relic is already owned must offer no relic, make no RNG request, and still offer
   Proceed and an eligible Sapphire, never a fault. Test
   `m24_act3_pools_exhausted_before_act3` (Task 2).
2. **Time Warp's twelfth card opens a selection.** When the twelfth card is ThirdEye
   (Scry), Meditate or a card that triggers a stance choice, the selection resolves under
   its real parent before the early end of turn, and no thirteenth card action appears.
   Test `m24_w02b_timewarp_twelfth_card_with_selection` (Task 5).
3. **Parasite against Falling and EmptyDeck.** Falling never captures Parasite (a Curse).
   With deck `[Parasite, X]`, Falling offers only X's type; removing X leaves `[Parasite]`
   and does **not** end the Run. Separately, with deck `[X]`, removing X ends the Run with
   EmptyDeck. Test `m24_falling_with_parasite_and_last_card` (Task 3).
4. **Tomb with zero gold.** With 0 gold and no mask, Tomb's offer still trades all (zero)
   gold for Red Mask exactly once, and the next combat starts with Red Mask. Test
   `m24_tomb_with_zero_gold` (Task 3).
5. **A cutoff slot in a batch.** A batch step that includes a slot at `Act4Ready` or at
   the `Act3NoKeys` terminal rejects the whole step before any peer changes; replaying an
   `Act4Ready` record ends at the same healthy cutoff. Test
   `m24_batch_rejects_step_with_cutoff_slot` (Task 7).
