# M27 relic and potion lifecycles Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development or
> superpowers:executing-plans to carry out this plan task by task, after the one independent
> plan review returns READY, M25 and M26 are merged and the PM releases execution. Steps use
> checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax. Use meaningful TDD and one fresh independent final milestone
> review; do not add per-task review swarms.

**Goal:** Make every standard relic and potion exist and work for every character that can hold
it, with complete obtain, equip, replacement, loss, pool, counter, use, rescue and escape
lifecycles across fights and rooms, one inventory and one relic dispatcher.

**Architecture:** One catalog-derived item capability (`content::complete` relic and potion
unions), a combat-context overlay (`with_complete_items()`, on top of M26's
`with_complete_cards()`) and a Run admission selector (`ItemAdmission::Complete`) beside the
frozen finite profiles. One acquisition-order relic dispatcher (`items/dispatch.rs`), one module
of modifier laws (`items/laws.rs`) and one module of Run entry points (`items/run_laws.rs`) call
per-relic handlers in lane-owned files. Evidence is constructed and supplied core tests; no
public profile changes.

**Tech Stack:** The existing locked Rust workspace (`spire-core`, `spire-env`) and its checked
Run RNG. No new dependency, no Python rebuild, no original-game execution.

**Spec:** [M27 design](../specs/2026-10-31-m27-items-design.md). "Design §N" refers to it; Appendix
A (per relic) and Appendix B (per potion) are part of it. The design pins every roster, law, value
and limit; this plan repeats them only where a step needs them inline.

## Review Focus

Five conditions the design implies but no section states as a test. Each has a test in its owning
task.

1. **Rejected item mutations leave the Run untouched.** Obtaining an owned non-Circlet relic,
   another class's relic, any relic during combat, Black Blood without Burning Blood, losing an
   unowned relic, buying a potion with Sozu, and claiming a potion reward with a full belt each
   return an error or "not obtained" with relics, potions, counters, deck, HP, maximum HP, gold and
   RNG counters unchanged. Test `m27_item_mutation_rejections_are_atomic` (Task M4).
2. **Rescue happens once, in source order.** At maximum HP 80, lethal damage with two Fairies and an
   unused Lizard Tail heals 24 from the first Fairy only (48 with Sacred Bark); a second lethal hit
   heals 24 from the second Fairy; a third heals 40 from Lizard Tail; a fourth kills. With Mark of the
   Bloom the first lethal hit kills. Toy Ornithopter never heals during a rescue. Test
   `m27_rescue_order_fairy_then_lizard_tail_once` (Task M5).
3. **Counters survive every combat end.** Pen Nib at 9 played to a Victory, an Escape (Smoke Bomb)
   and a Death: after Victory and Escape the next combat's first Attack deals double damage and the
   counter resets; after Death the Run terminal settles without a fault and the inventory returns
   once. Combat-local state (Kunai's count, Centennial Puzzle's flag) never reaches the Run. Test
   `m27_counters_persist_through_every_combat_end` (Task M4).
4. **Energy law for every character.** For each character with 3 base energy: Sozu alone gives 4
   (correction G1); Sozu with Busted Crown gives 5; Slaver's Collar adds 1 only in elite and boss
   combats; Ice Cream with 2 unspent energy at end of turn gives 5 next turn (3 + 2), through each
   character's own recharge owner; with the Watcher's Fasting the `energy_down` subtracts after the
   carry; the 999 cap holds. Test `m27_energy_law_all_characters` (Task M5).
5. **Necronomicon with other duplicators.** A 2-cost Attack with Necronomicon plays twice once per
   turn; with Double Tap active it plays three times (Double Tap once, Necronomicon once) with energy
   paid once; a 1-cost Attack is not repeated; an X-cost Attack with X ≥ 2 follows binding F9; the
   second qualifying Attack in the same turn is not repeated. Test
   `m27_necronomicon_with_duplicators` (Task J1).
