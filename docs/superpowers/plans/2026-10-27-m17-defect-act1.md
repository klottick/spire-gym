1. **Wrong or stale upstream/profile/history:** bind accepted M16 mechanics and typed initial Act1 history before construction/reset; foreign/partial/duplicate histories fail without mutating a live session. Task0 receipt; Task4 `m17_history_is_typed_strict_and_profile_bound`.

Task1 `m17_finite_rows_cursors_and_belt_keep_shared_custody`; Task2 `m17_child_and_transition_faults_never_repeat_accepted_work`.
3. **Persistent versus combat-local Defect state:** GA misc survives upgrades/settlement/Death/accepted-prefix fault once; orb state, temporary costs and card mutations reset according to M16. Task2 `m17_permanent_misc_and_combat_local_state_have_distinct_lifetimes`.
