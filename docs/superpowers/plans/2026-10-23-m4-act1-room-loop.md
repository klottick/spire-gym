## Review Focus

1. Profile honesty and privacy: normal resources must not hide a reduced standard random pool; unrevealed question payloads, chest contents and unopened card choices stay private, while normal/M3 behavior remains available unchanged.
2. Paid/cancellable versus committed selections: no payment on shop-grid opening/cancel, one payment/removal on accepted pick; shrine removal has no invented post-commit escape, Smith cancellation returns to its actual campfire, and stale captured identities cannot select a later parent.
3. Offer/resource ownership: full potion belts, sold/exclusive rows, repeated claims and next-card IDs cannot spend/drop/refund/duplicate resources. Last-hit settlement prepares follow-up loot before releasing its terminal combat; faults cannot retry entry, reward preparation or acquisition.
4. Real lifecycle effects: short Neow alternatives, Lament counter3→2→1→−2, native HP1/maxHP unchanged, permanent upgrades/removals, Rest healing and Strawberry equip must execute once at the right checkpoint, not merely register an ID or log a hook.
5. Reward/outcome accounting: highest-floor/key/terminal contributions are credited once from committed events; purchases, gold loot, reads, invalid actions, operational faults and an unfinished route are not extra reward or fabricated victories/deaths.
