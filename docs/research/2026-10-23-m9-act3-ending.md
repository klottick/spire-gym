## Reward construction is not reward publication

`AbstractRoom.endBattle` constructs the generic boss gold object at offsets599–651
(non-daily A0 range95–105). Its later non-endless `MonsterRoomBoss`/`TheBeyond`
branch at1018–1051 exits at1214, **before** `dropReward`, `addPotionToRewards` and
`CombatRewardScreen.open`. Therefore the final Act3 boss does not present claimable
gold, card or potion rows; constructing an unpresented object does not award gold.
The potion roll/pity update is also bypassed. Native M9 does not manufacture that
reward screen or consume an unrelated native gold/potion stream for invisible loot.

This finding follows the publication chain, not absence of a direct card call:
`CombatRewardScreen.open` calls `setupItemReward`; that later owner can append a
card reward on paths that actually open the screen. Earlier general-boss/card
inferences from `endBattle` alone were incomplete and are superseded here. Existing
earlier milestone offers remain their explicitly declared captures; no unrelated
pool/rarity audit or implementation change is implied.

## Actual A0 Act3 branch and ending decisions

`ProceedButton.update`'s boss/`TheBeyond` branch179–232 chooses the A20 second-boss
path only under its additional conditions; the ordinary A0 non-endless path calls
`goToVictoryRoomOrTheDoor`. That method queues `VictoryRoom(EventType.HEART)`.
`VictoryRoom.onPlayerEntry`0–66 hides Proceed, constructs `SpireHeart` and invokes
its entry callback. Generic combat-reward/treasure-button branches elsewhere in
`ProceedButton.update` do not establish a third Act3 boss chest: the Act3 path
above never opened `COMBAT_REWARD` in the first place.

`SpireHeart` constructor selects INTRO and one dialog option. Its `buttonEffect`
has the following accepted-choice progression:

1. INTRO → MIDDLE (44–77).
2. MIDDLE → MIDDLE_2 (80–140).
3. MIDDLE_2 checks final-act availability and all resolved Ruby/Emerald/Sapphire
   flags (305–428). Success selects GO_TO_ENDING; otherwise DEATH (431–608).
4. GO_TO_ENDING's next choice calls `goToFinalAct` (651–674), which opens the door
   screen. DEATH's next choice sets dying/dead and creates DeathScreen (611–648).

The door is not automatically opened on event entry or the third choice. No-key
ending still has a fourth accepted dialog choice; do not silently auto-select it.
Localized dialog/animation/statistic presentation is outside this headless profile.
