## Finite boundary

Canonical Watcher A0 starts at short Neow with 72/72 HP, 99 gold, the ten starter instances, PureWater and three empty potion slots. The profile replays the exact M22 Act1 prefix, continues through the shared captured Act2 (floors 18–32, boss 33, boss treasure 34) and stops at healthy nonterminal `Act3Ready` (act 3, floor 34, zero legal actions, no readiness reward) or at a genuine Death or EmptyDeck terminal. Act3 boss icons (AwakenedOne, TimeEater, DonuAndDeca) are map metadata only. Act3/Act4 play, A1–20, full Neow, other Watcher content, standard pools, Python delivery and original-runtime validation are outside this profile.

Card rewards use the full global `CombatRecord.visit` (mod 6 rows, mod 7 potions) across the act boundary. Merchant stock is selected by the destination act's visit count before increment. Relic capture uses the single scoped helper: Act1 keeps `run.m22.relic.capture`, and Act2 elites/chests use `run.m23.relic.capture` with key `[act,floor,node_id,full_visit,source_tag]`. Ownership filtering happens before any request, an empty pool makes no request, and a singleton still makes one checked request. Act2 questions are AncientWriting (purple Simplicity upgrades base StrikePurple/DefendPurple), then Joust at gold ≥ 50 or Purifier, then captured Monster rooms. The boss treasure uses the M22 five-relic menu and the shared transition owner.

Under a matched creation history, the M23 prefix equals the M22 run exactly, including RNG, captures, records and the successor map. The only differences are the scenario tag and the continuation boundary. Native M22's all-seen Act2 history draws boss-order shuffle tickets, so unmatched native runs diverge after successor selection. This is disclosed, not normalized.

## Scope of the outcome evidence

The primary scope of this repository is the ability to play the game, not strategy. Winning with the code in this repository is not in scope, and a supplied victory is acceptable gameplay validation (primary clarification, 2026-10-30). The canonical cases use a fixed simple public policy. Whether they reach any Act2 boss victory or healthy Act3Ready is a policy outcome, reported as observed. It is not a profile gap or a blocker. The supplied boss series and the played and constructed diagnostics validate gameplay, custody, fault and progression mechanics. Their provenance stays exact: supplied resources and constructed sources are labelled as such and never imply normal traversal. Mechanics, API, custody, fault or progression defects remain must-fix regardless of policy results.
