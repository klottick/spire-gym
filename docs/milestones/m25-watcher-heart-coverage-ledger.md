# M25 Watcher A0 coverage ledger

Candidate ledger for the [M25 profile](m25-watcher-heart.md). It has one row per item in the profile's
unions: the 75 ordinary Watcher cards, 8 generated children, 3 Wish options, 106 Foreign
Influence outputs, 23 relics, 7 potions, the 7 reachable events and the 18 design §10
compositions. Each row names the constructed tests that play, trigger or assert the item through the
shared owners (design §12.2 keys). It also names the retained once-only receipts in which the item was
actually played or triggered. Grades are never merged: a test is **constructed** evidence;
a receipt is **normal played** (canonical, freeze v1) or **supplied played** (freeze v1 traversal
and Act4 A; freeze v2 Act4 B, strong and fixtures; freeze v3 Act4 C. The freeze-v1 C receipt
after step 25 is pre-correction history and is not cited. "none" in the played column means the item
was never played in a retained receipt and is pinned by its constructed tests only.

Keys: G generation, D direct values, S state, P powers, F Foreign Influence, C compositions
(`m25_watcher_interactions*`, `m25_watcher_nested_autoplay`, `m25_watcher_selection_during_play`),
I items, A Run-level content, R Run/Heart, N native (`crates/spire-env/tests/run_m25_*`).

## Known constructed-coverage gaps

Source review found no item without an exercising constructed test. The purple Foreign Influence
outputs (ordinary Watcher Attacks) have support-membership assertions and their own card tests, but
no test forces each one through the Foreign Influence offer path; the 82 non-Watcher outputs are each
played by a Watcher through that path (key F). This is recorded, not hidden.
